# Disha Dira booking assistant: v2 design

Version: 2026-10-21 (rev 2, after independent review). Status: draft for Rohit's approval. CONTRACT.md v4 summarises the binding parts; this file has the detail.

## 1. What we are building, from first principles

**The job.** Turn people who message from an ad into paid ₹99 calls with Shantha Rao. She only ever speaks to people who have paid. The ₹700 session is offered by Amma on the call, not by the bot.

**Who actually messages.** In rough order of how often:

| Who | What they want | What the bot does |
|---|---|---|
| Curious first-timers | "What is pranic healing?", "How does it work on the phone?" | Answer briefly and warmly, then offer the ₹99 call |
| People going through something hard (stress, family trouble, feeling stuck, grief) | To be heard and to know if this can help | Acknowledge, never diagnose, explain what a call is, offer it |
| Skeptics | "Is this real? Any proof? Is it a scam?" | Be honest: complementary, research is limited, no promises; ₹99 is a low-risk way to decide |
| Price shoppers | Discounts, free sessions | Prices are fixed; the ₹99 call is the low-risk start; the refund rule |
| Paying customers | Payment, reschedule, "when is my call" | Never filtered. Use the booking data, or hand to Rohit |
| People in crisis | Talk of ending their life, or a medical emergency | Fixed safety message to everyone (even blocked), alert Rohit at once, hand the chat to a person, no selling |
| Noise | Timepass, flirting, abuse, spam, jokes, "send pic", forwards | Strikes, then a polite close and silence (section 4) |
| Wrong-fit requests | Vashikaran, "get my love back", harming or controlling someone, horoscopes, lottery numbers, curses | A clear, polite no. Repeats count as strikes |
| Learners and job seekers | "Do you teach?", "Can I work with you?" | Hand to Rohit |

**What has to be true:**
1. **Money stays deterministic.** The AI never creates holds, links, refunds or bookings. It only picks a next *action* from a fixed list. Code does the rest, through the existing guarded steps.
2. **Every AI reply is one WhatsApp message.** The reply text is the body. At most one button is attached: "See call times" or "Talk to a person". One message keeps one Meta charge.
3. **Nothing the bot says can be a health claim.**
   - It never names an illness, promises results, gives medical advice or tells anyone to change treatment.
   - It never offers to remove, counter or confirm anything supernatural, and never says "black magic" or "evil eye", even when the customer does.
   - After a disease is named, it never pushes a booking in the same reply.
4. **Noise costs money.** Since 1 Oct 2026, Meta charges for every bot reply in India (about ₹0.115 each). The exception is the free window that opens when someone comes from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad. The LLM costs a little too. Silence is the cheapest reply to a time-waster.
5. **A person can always take over.** Meta policy requires a clear route to a human. "Talk to a person" always works, even during a quiet period. It puts the chat in Rohit's queue.
6. **The bot says what it is.** It's Disha Dira's automated assistant, and Shantha Rao calls personally.
7. **Safety beats everything.** Crisis and emergency detection runs first, on every inbound text, whatever the chat's state.

## 2. Languages

- **English and Hindi.** Kannada is removed.
- The first message is bilingual, with two buttons: **English** and **हिंदी**.
- **Fixed messages** (greeting, menu, booking, payment, confirmations, reminders, safety messages, fallbacks) are written once in each language. They live in `src/copy.ts`, with text from KNOWLEDGE.md §11, and are never generated by the AI.
- **AI replies** mirror the customer's script:
  - Devanagari in, Devanagari out.
  - Roman Hinglish in, Roman Hinglish out.
  - English in, English out.
  - Detection runs in code first: any Devanagari character means Hindi script. The AI reports the language as well.
- **Call language** = the chat language, so there is no extra booking step. The confirmation says "Call in Hindi" or "Call in English", and Amma's page shows it. To change it, the customer uses "Change language".
- **Templates** (outside the 24-hour window) need Hindi versions approved by Meta. Until they are approved, Hindi customers get the English template.

## 3. The conversation flow

```
Any inbound text ──► SAFETY CHECK (always first)
                          │ (not crisis/emergency)
                          ▼
First message? ──► Greeting (bilingual) + [English] [हिंदी]
                   (a real question in it is kept and answered after the language tap)
                          ▼
              Menu: [Book ₹99 call] [How it works] [Talk to a person]
                     │                 │                 │
   typed question ───┼──► GATEKEEPER ──► AI ──► output check ──► one message (+ ≤1 button)
                     ▼
            Booking (fixed, no AI):
            age check (once) → service → who for → day → time
            → hold + Razorpay link → paid → confirmation
```

### 3.1 Booking (fixed messages only)
1. **Age check, once per person, at the first booking:**
   - "Are you 18 or older?" with buttons [Yes] [No].
   - No: "Please ask a parent or guardian to message us. They can book the call for you."
2. **Service:** [Healing] [Energy cleansing].
   - "Energy cleansing" is the customer-facing name for the PROTECTION service, which stays the internal code.
   - This is pending Rohit's confirmation; see §8.
3. **Who for:** [Me] [Family member] [Pet]. Stored on the intro and shown on Amma's page. Children are booked by a parent, who is on the call.
4. **Day:** up to 3 buttons, e.g. "Wed, 21 Oct", for days inside the booking window that still have a free slot. Days with none are skipped.
5. **Time:** a list for that day.
   - At most 9 times, in time order: prefer :00 and :30, and fill a taken half hour with :15 or :45. Drop the latest ones if there are more than 9.
   - A final "Another day" row.
   - Sections: Afternoon (before 4 pm) and Evening. The "Another day" row goes in the last non-empty section, and empty sections are never sent.
6. **Payment:** the message names the day and time held, then a [Pay ₹99] button. Everything after that already exists.

### 3.2 When a text arrives: the order of checks
1. **Safety** (code, not AI): English, Hindi and Hinglish phrase lists (KNOWLEDGE.md §9), after normalising.
   - **Crisis:**
     - Send the fixed crisis message. This happens even if the person is blocked, in HUMAN mode, quiet, over budget, or writing their first-ever message.
     - Raise an URGENT attention item and email Rohit at once.
     - Set the chat to HUMAN and `no_sell_until` = now + 7 days.
     - Exempt from burst skip.
   - **Medical emergency:** send the fixed emergency message and raise attention.
   - Both run even when the SENDS *switch* is paused. They do not run when the SENDS *env* flag is "false", which only happens on a test or unreleased Worker.
2. **Blocked?** No reply, apart from step 1.
3. **First message ever?** Greeting. If the text was a real question (not a menu word), keep it and answer it after the language tap.
4. **Opt-out words** ("stop", "unsubscribe", "बंद करो", "band karo"):
   - Mark the person opted out and confirm once.
   - No templates are sent to them after that.
   - If they write again later, normal replies resume and the opt-out clears.
5. **"Talk to a person"** (button or words like "human", "person", "insaan", "इंसान"): always honoured, even when quiet. HUMAN mode, attention item, handoff ack. At most one open handoff item per person.
6. **HUMAN mode:**
   - Booking and menu button taps still work.
   - Free text gets no bot reply. It waits for Rohit in /admin.
   - Resolving the item returns the chat to BOT.
7. **Paying customer:** someone with a PAID, CONFIRMED or RESCHEDULED booking, or a PAID payment, in the last 30 days.
   - Skips strikes and quiet.
   - Gets a higher AI cap (40 replies a day).
   - Their questions reach the AI with their booking facts.
   - Payment, refund or reschedule trouble goes straight to Rohit.
   - A hold alone does not make someone a paying customer.
8. **Quiet period active?**
   - Free text gets no reply.
   - Booking buttons and the word "book" / "बुक" still work: they open the booking flow, but they don't end the quiet period.
   - Payment ends it.
9. **Non-text** (voice note, image, video, sticker, document, location, contact):
   - If the person has a hold, a pending payment or a booking, raise an attention item, because it may be a payment screenshot, and send the notice.
   - Otherwise the first one each day gets the notice ("Please type your question, or tap a button"). Later ones get no reply.
   - Media is never downloaded.
10. **Short acknowledgements** ("ok", "thanks", "thank you", "👍", "🙏", "ji", "धन्यवाद", "शुक्रिया"): no reply, no strike, no AI.
11. **Menu words** (hi, hello, menu, start, namaste, नमस्ते, …): show the menu. No AI.
12. **Budget:** if this person has reached their daily AI cap, or the global daily cap is used up, send the menu with "Talk to a person". No AI.
13. **The AI** returns a label and a reply (section 6).
14. **Apply the label** (section 4), then run the output check, then send.

### 3.3 Burst handling
Before replying to a typed message, check whether a newer inbound message exists from the same person. If it does, skip this reply, because the newer message's handler answers using the recent history.
- Safety replies are never skipped.
- The safety check runs on every inbound message, including ones whose reply is skipped.

## 4. Noise filter ("Balanced", approved by Rohit)

**Labels** (from the AI, applied by code):

| Label | Effect | Bot response |
|---|---|---|
| `relevant_question`, `personal_concern`, `objection`, `booking_intent`, `existing_booking`, `human_request` | None | Normal answer |
| `smalltalk` | The first two a day are free; after that, a strike each | Short friendly line plus the menu |
| `off_topic` (cricket, jokes, homework, coding, general knowledge) | Strike | "I can only help with Disha Dira calls and bookings." |
| `astrology_or_prediction` | Strike | "We don't do astrology or predictions." |
| `prohibited_request` (control, attract or harm a person, "love back", vashikaran, revenge, curses) | Strike | Firm, kind refusal |
| `loop` (same question 3 times after an answer) | Strike | One-line restatement |
| `spam_or_promo` | Strike, no reply | none |
| `flirt_or_sexual` | Strike plus one line, "This chat is only for bookings." It becomes a block only if the text also matches the code word list, or on a second offence. | |
| `abuse` | Strike. Block if the text also matches the code word list, or on a second offence. | No reply |

**Three strikes:**
1. Send one closing line: "It seems we can't help with this. If you'd like to book a call with Shantha Rao later, just type *book*."
2. Start a 7-day quiet period (§3.2 step 8).
3. A **second quiet period within 30 days becomes a block.**

**Strikes clear** only when the person pays, or 7 days after the last strike.

**Blocks** use the existing `blocks` table (reason `NOISE_ABUSE`, `NOISE_SEXUAL` or `NOISE_REPEAT`), so Rohit can unblock from /admin. Blocks, quiet periods and budget fallbacks appear in the 9pm digest as counts.

**Seriousness signals for Rohit** (never for gating): tapped Book; asked about price, timing or the process; described a concern; started a hold but didn't pay. The digest lists "warm leads": people with an expired hold or a booking intent in the last 24 hours and no payment. It shows an /admin link per person and no message text, so Rohit can follow up within the 24-hour window.

## 5. Safety messages (fixed, both languages; exact text in KNOWLEDGE.md §9)
- **Crisis:**
  - Warm, short acknowledgement.
  - Tele-MANAS **14416** or **1-800-891-4416**: a free government helpline, open 24×7.
  - **112** if in immediate danger.
  - "A person from our team has been told and will message you here as soon as they can."
  - Then the chat goes to HUMAN mode, with no selling for 7 days.
- **Medical emergency:** "Please call **112**, or **108** for an ambulance, now, or go to the nearest hospital."
- **AI-labelled crisis** (no code phrase matched): the same fixed message, at most once per person per day. The AI's own text is dropped.

## 6. The AI part

**Model:** Claude Haiku 5.5 through the Anthropic API.
- Published price today: $0.10 in, $0.50 out, $0.01 cache read and $0.125 cache write per million tokens.
- The model id and prices go in config.

**Cost sketch** (per AI reply, ~8k-token knowledge bank):

| Item | Cost per reply |
|---|---|
| Knowledge bank, cache hit | ~₹0.01 |
| Knowledge bank, cache miss (likely at low traffic) | ~₹0.08 |
| New tokens in (~1.5k) and out (~300) | ~₹0.03 |
| **AI total** | **₹0.04 to ₹0.11** |
| WhatsApp delivery (free inside the 72-hour ad window) | ₹0.115 |

That's about ₹1 to ₹1.50 per 6-reply conversation. Some providers report the first 1,000 service messages a month are free; Meta's own page doesn't confirm it, so don't count on it.

**Caps:**
- 12 AI replies per person per day (40 for paying customers).
- Global cap in config, starting at 300 a day.
- Over a cap, the bot falls back to buttons.
- Flag `AI_REPLIES` (default "false") works like SENDS: the env allows it, and /admin can pause it.

**What the AI receives:**
- System part 1 (cached): rules plus KNOWLEDGE.md.
- System part 2 (not cached), the trusted facts:
  - today's date in India
  - the chat language
  - booking facts: next call or session day and time, and status
  - the `no_sell` flag
- Messages: the last 10 messages of the chat. Before sending, code:
  - replaces digit runs of 6 or more, emails and links with `[removed]`
  - strips anything that looks like a tag (`<…>`) from customer text
  - never sends the phone number, the WhatsApp name or payment ids

**What the AI must return** (one forced tool call, checked with zod; anything invalid falls back):

```json
{
  "label": "relevant_question | personal_concern | objection | booking_intent | existing_booking | human_request | smalltalk | off_topic | astrology_or_prediction | prohibited_request | loop | spam_or_promo | flirt_or_sexual | abuse | crisis | medical_emergency",
  "language": "en | hi",
  "script": "latin | devanagari",
  "reply": "text, max 600 characters, no links, no phone numbers",
  "next": "show_booking | show_menu | ask_more | handoff | none"
}
```

**How `next` is sent** (one message each):

| `next` | Sent as |
|---|---|
| `show_booking` | Reply as body + [See call times]. When `no_sell` is set, the reply only. |
| `show_menu` | Reply as the menu message's body. When `no_sell` is set, the menu without the Book button. |
| `handoff` | Reply, then HUMAN mode and attention, at most one open item per person |
| `ask_more` / `none` | Reply only |

**Output check (code, after the AI).** Before matching, code normalises the text:
- lowercase it
- NFC, strip the nukta, and map ँ to ं
- turn Devanagari digits into 0–9

The reply is replaced by the fixed safe fallback for its label (KNOWLEDGE.md §11, "Fallbacks") if any of these hold:
- It contains a banned word or phrase (KNOWLEDGE.md §8), matched as a **whole word or phrase**, so "secure" and "spiritual" pass.
- It contains a URL, an email or a phone-like number.
- It contains a number that isn't on the allowlist:
  - prices, durations, hours and the hold minutes
  - 18, 24, 30, 120 and 1980
  - numbers that appear in today's booking facts
- It contains characters outside Latin, Devanagari, common punctuation and the allowed emoji.
- It is over 600 characters.
- It mentions "system prompt", "instructions" or "Anthropic".

**Prompt injection:**
- Customer text is data, never instructions.
- Trusted facts come only from system part 2, so a customer typing a fake booking block changes nothing.
- An AI-labelled `crisis` or `handoff` is rate-limited (above), so injected labels can't flood Rohit.

**When the API fails or times out (8 s):** send `aiFallback` plus the menu. The same message is never retried.

**Vendor and privacy:**
- Anthropic processes the text in the US.
- The greeting and the privacy page disclose the automated assistant and the AI provider (KNOWLEDGE.md §10).
- Chat text is still deleted 30 days after the last activity.

**Meta policy:** this is a business-specific assistant for one practice's bookings and questions, which is allowed under the 15 Jan 2026 rules (only general-purpose assistants are banned). The AI refuses general-knowledge chat (`off_topic`), which keeps it on the right side of that line.

## 7. Before go-live: checks Rohit runs
1. **The eval set** (`test/agent-evals/`): every scenario in KNOWLEDGE.md §7.
   - CI runs it with a fake AI.
   - The real model runs by hand, in a manual GitHub workflow.

2. **Shadow week:** the AI runs on the test number only. Rohit reads every reply and the digest.
3. **Amma reads** KNOWLEDGE.md and the Hindi fixed messages, and fixes anything that doesn't sound like her.
