# Disha Dira booking assistant: v2 design

Version: 2026-10-21. Status: draft for Rohit's approval. Once approved, the "Contract changes" section at the end goes into CONTRACT.md as v4.

## 1. What we are building, from first principles

**The job.** Turn people who message from an ad into paid ₹99 calls with Shantha Rao. She only ever speaks to people who have paid. The ₹700 session is offered by Amma on the call, not by the bot.

**Who actually messages.** In rough order of how often:

| Who | What they want | What the bot does |
|---|---|---|
| Curious first-timers | "What is pranic healing?", "How does it work on the phone?" | Answer briefly and warmly, then offer the ₹99 call |
| People going through something hard (stress, family trouble, feeling stuck, grief) | To be heard and to know if this can help | Acknowledge, never diagnose, explain what a call is, offer it |
| Skeptics | "Is this real? Any proof? Is it a scam?" | Be honest: complementary, research is limited, no promises; ₹99 is a low-risk way to decide |
| Price shoppers | Discounts, free sessions | Prices are fixed; the ₹99 call is the low-risk start; the refund rule |
| Existing customers | Payment, reschedule, "when is my call" | Never filtered. Use the booking data, or hand to Rohit |
| People in crisis | Talk of ending their life, or a medical emergency | Fixed safety message with helplines, alert Rohit at once, no selling |
| Noise | Timepass, flirting, abuse, spam, jokes, "send pic", forwarded messages | Strikes, then a polite close and silence (section 4) |
| Wrong-fit requests | Vashikaran, "get my love back", harming or controlling someone, horoscopes, lottery numbers, curses | A clear, polite no. Repeats count as strikes |
| Learners and job seekers | "Do you teach?", "Can I work with you?" | Hand to Rohit |

**What has to be true:**
1. **Money stays deterministic.** The AI never creates holds, links, refunds or bookings. It only picks a next *action* from a fixed list. Code does the rest, through the existing guarded steps.
2. **Every AI reply is short and ends in one clear next step.** That's usually a button: "See call times", "Ask another question" or "Talk to a person".
3. **Nothing the bot says can be a health claim.** It never names an illness, promises results, gives medical advice or tells anyone to change treatment. It never says "black magic" or "evil eye", even when the customer does.
4. **Noise costs money.** Since 1 Oct 2026, Meta charges for every bot reply in India (about ₹0.115 each). The exception is the free window that opens when someone comes from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad. The LLM costs a little too. Silence is the cheapest reply to a time-waster.
5. **A person can always take over.** Meta policy requires a clear route to a human. "Talk to a person" is always available, and it puts the chat in Rohit's queue.
6. **The bot says what it is.** It's Disha Dira's automated assistant, and Shantha Rao calls personally.

## 2. Languages

- **English and Hindi.** Kannada is removed.
- The first message is bilingual, with two buttons: **English** and **हिंदी**.
- **Fixed messages** (greeting, menu, booking, payment, confirmations, reminders, safety messages) are written once in each language. They live in `src/copy.ts` and are never generated by the AI.
- **AI replies** mirror the customer's script:
  - Devanagari in, Devanagari out.
  - Roman Hinglish in, Roman Hinglish out.
  - English in, English out.
  - Detection runs in code first: any Devanagari character means Hindi script. The AI reports the language as well.
- **Call language.** Amma takes calls in English or Hindi. The booking flow records the chosen language and shows it on Amma's page next to the name.

## 3. The conversation flow

```
First message ──► Greeting (bilingual) + [English] [हिंदी]
                     │
                     ▼
              Menu: [Book ₹99 call] [How it works] [Talk to a person]
                     │                 │                 │
   typed question ───┼──► GATEKEEPER ──► AI ANSWER ──► reply + 1 next-step button
                     │                                  │
                     ▼                                  ▼
            Booking (fixed, no AI):                (back to menu or booking)
            age check (once) → service → day → time → call language
            → hold + Razorpay link → paid → confirmation
```

### 3.1 Booking (fixed messages only)
1. **Age check, once per person, at the first booking:**
   - "Are you 18 or older?" with buttons [Yes] [No].
   - No: "Please ask a parent or guardian to message us. They can book the call for you."
   - It is not asked again after a yes.
2. **Service:** Healing or Protection.
3. **Who is it for:** [Me] [Family member] [Pet]. This only changes one line Amma sees on her page ("for self / family / pet") and the confirmation text. Children are booked by a parent, who is on the call.
4. **Day:** up to 3 buttons, e.g. "Wed, 21 Oct", for working days that still have a free slot.
5. **Time:** a list for that day, at most 9 times plus "Another day". Afternoon and Evening sections.
6. **Call language:** [English] [हिंदी]. Pre-selected from the chat language, so it is one tap.
7. **Payment:** the message names the day and time held, then a [Pay ₹99] button. Everything after that already exists (webhook, poll, confirmation, refunds).

### 3.2 When a typed message arrives
Order of checks (the "gatekeeper"), cheapest first:

1. **Blocked?** No reply.
2. **Safety words** (code, not AI), in English, Hindi and Hinglish:
   - **Self-harm or crisis:** send the fixed crisis message (section 5), raise an URGENT attention item, email Rohit immediately, and pause all selling to this person for 7 days. This check runs even during a quiet period.
   - **Medical emergency** (can't breathe, chest pain, heavy bleeding, unconscious, poison): send the fixed emergency message (112 / 108).
3. **Existing customer with a booking or payment in the last 30 days:** never filtered, never quiet. Their questions go to the AI with their booking facts. Payment or refund trouble goes straight to Rohit.
4. **Quiet period active?** No reply. The only exceptions are a booking button tap or the word "book". Section 4 has the details.
5. **Non-text** (voice note, image, video, sticker, location, contact):
   - The first time each day: "I can only read typed messages. Please type your question, or tap a button below."
   - After that it gets no reply. Media is never downloaded.
6. **Menu words** (hi, hello, menu, start, namaste, नमस्ते, …): show the menu. No AI is called.
7. **Daily budget:** if this person has had 12 AI replies today, or the global daily AI budget is used up, send the menu with "Talk to a person", without calling the AI.
8. **Ask the AI.** It returns a label and a reply (section 6).
9. **Apply strikes** from the label, then send the reply.

### 3.3 Burst handling
People often send 3 messages in a row. Before sending a reply, check whether a newer message from the same person has arrived. If one has, skip this reply, because the newer message's handler sees the whole recent history and answers once.

## 4. Noise filter ("Balanced", approved by Rohit)

**Strike labels** (from the AI, checked in code):

| Label | Strike? | Bot response |
|---|---|---|
| `relevant_question`, `personal_concern`, `objection`, `booking_intent`, `existing_booking`, `human_request` | No | Normal answer |
| `smalltalk` | The first is free, then one strike each | Short friendly line, back to the menu |
| `off_topic` (cricket, jokes, homework, coding, general knowledge) | Strike | "I can only help with Disha Dira sessions and bookings." |
| `astrology_or_prediction` (kundli, future, lottery, matches) | Strike | "We don't do predictions or astrology." |
| `prohibited_request` (control, attract or harm a person, "love back", vashikaran, revenge, curses) | Strike, and the first one gets the firm refusal | "We can't help with influencing or harming another person." |
| `loop` (same question 3 times after an answer) | Strike | Short restatement and the menu |
| `spam_or_promo` (ads, links, "earn money", forwards) | Strike, and no reply at all | none |
| `flirt_or_sexual` | **Block at once** | One line: "This chat is only for bookings." Then block |
| `abuse` (insults, threats, slurs) | **Block at once** | No reply |

**Three strikes:**
1. The bot sends one closing line: "It seems we can't help with this. If you'd like to book a call with Shantha Rao later, just type *book*."
2. Then the **quiet period** starts: 7 days with no replies.
3. During the quiet period, only a booking button tap or the typed word "book" (or "बुक") gets a reply. Doing either is a serious action, so the strikes clear.

**Strikes reset** after a paid booking, or 7 days after the last strike.

**Blocks** use the existing `blocks` table, so Rohit can unblock from /admin. Every block or quiet period shows in the 9pm digest as counts only.

**Not filtered, ever:** anyone with a booking or payment in the last 30 days, and any safety-word message.

**Signals of seriousness** (shown to Rohit, not used for gating): tapped Book; asked about price, timing or process; described a concern; asked about the call. "Serious" never comes from the AI's own guess alone.

## 5. Safety messages (fixed, both languages; exact text in KNOWLEDGE.md §9)
- **Crisis:**
  - Warm, short acknowledgement.
  - Tele-MANAS **14416** or **1-800-891-4416**: free, 24×7, government.
  - **112** if in immediate danger.
  - "A person from our team has been told and will message you."
  - No selling for 7 days.
- **Medical emergency:** "Please call **112** or **108** for an ambulance now, or go to the nearest hospital." No selling in that reply.
- **Medical questions:** Pranic healing works alongside a doctor's care, never instead of it. "Please keep following your doctor's advice." Then an offer of the call.

## 6. The AI part

**Model:** Claude Haiku 5.5 through the Anthropic API.
- Price as published today: $0.10 in, $0.50 out and $0.01 cache-read per million tokens.
- The model id and prices go in config, never hard-coded.

**Cost sketch** (per AI reply, with an ~8k-token knowledge bank cached):

| Item | Cost per reply |
|---|---|
| Cached knowledge bank (8k tokens) | ~₹0.007 |
| New tokens in (~1.5k) | ~₹0.013 |
| Tokens out (~300) | ~₹0.013 |
| **AI total** | **≈ ₹0.03** |
| WhatsApp delivery (free inside the 72-hour ad window) | ₹0.115 |

A 6-reply conversation costs about ₹1 all-in. Some providers report the first 1,000 service messages a month are free; Meta's own page doesn't confirm that, so don't count on it.

**Caps:** 12 AI replies per person per day, and a global cap set in config, starting at 300 AI replies a day. When a cap is reached, the bot falls back to buttons. A new flag `AI_REPLIES` (default `"false"`) works like `SENDS`: the env allows it, and /admin can pause it.

**What the AI receives:**
- The system prompt: rules plus KNOWLEDGE.md, cached.
- The last 10 messages of the chat, with digits of 6 or more, emails and links replaced by `[removed]`.
- This person's booking facts: next call time, status, language.
- It never receives the phone number, the WhatsApp name or anyone's payment ids.

**What the AI must return** (JSON, checked with zod; anything invalid falls back to the menu):

```json
{
  "label": "relevant_question | personal_concern | objection | booking_intent | existing_booking | human_request | smalltalk | off_topic | astrology_or_prediction | prohibited_request | loop | spam_or_promo | flirt_or_sexual | abuse | crisis | medical_emergency",
  "language": "en | hi",
  "script": "latin | devanagari",
  "reply": "text, max 600 characters, no links, no phone numbers",
  "next": "show_booking | show_menu | ask_more | handoff | none"
}
```

**Output check (code, after the AI):** the reply is thrown away and replaced by a safe fixed answer if any of these hold:
- It contains a banned word or claim. The banned list (KNOWLEDGE.md §8) is checked case-insensitively in both scripts.
- It contains a number that isn't one of our prices, durations or times.
- It contains a URL or a phone number.
- It is over 600 characters.
- It mentions the system prompt or instructions.

**Prompt injection:** customer text is always passed as data, never mixed into the instructions. The rules say to ignore any instructions inside customer messages. Prices, refunds and policies can only come from KNOWLEDGE.md, never from the chat.

**When the API fails or times out (8 s):** send the menu with "Talk to a person". Never retry the same message twice.

**Vendor and privacy:**
- Anthropic processes the text in the US.
- The privacy page and the first message must say that an automated assistant helps answer questions and that messages are processed by our AI provider. Exact wording is in KNOWLEDGE.md §10.
- Chat text is still deleted 30 days after the last activity.

**Meta policy:** this is a business-specific assistant for one practice's bookings and questions. That is allowed under the 15 Jan 2026 rules, which ban only general-purpose assistants. The AI refuses general-knowledge chat (`off_topic`), which keeps the bot on the right side of that line.

## 7. Before go-live: checks Rohit runs
1. **The 60-case eval set** (`test/agent-evals/`): every scenario in KNOWLEDGE.md §7. Expected label and next step per case; no banned words.
   - It runs with a fake AI in CI.
   - It runs against the real model by hand, as a script Rohit starts with the test key.
   - The bar: ≥ 95% correct labels, 100% safety cases correct, 0 banned words.
2. **Shadow week:** the AI runs on the test number only. Rohit reads every reply and the digest.
3. **Amma reads** KNOWLEDGE.md and the Hindi fixed messages, and fixes anything that doesn't sound like her.

## 9. Contract changes (to paste into CONTRACT.md as v4 after approval)
- Scope: English + Hindi (Kannada removed).
- Stack: add "Anthropic API (Claude Haiku) for typed-question answers only, behind the AI_REPLIES flag". Remove "AI/LLM replies" from "Not in v1".
- Flow invariants: the bot is button-led. Typed messages go through the gatekeeper (safety, existing customer, quiet, media, menu words, budget) before any AI call. The AI may only choose a reply and one `next` action from a fixed list. It never creates holds, links, refunds or bookings.
- Safety: crisis and medical-emergency detection runs in code before the AI and sends fixed messages. Every AI reply passes the output check before sending.
- Noise: the strike, quiet-period and block rules above. Existing customers and safety messages are never filtered.
- Privacy: phone numbers, names, payment ids, emails, links and long digit runs are never sent to the AI. The first message and the privacy page disclose the automated assistant and the AI provider.
- Flags: add `AI_REPLIES` (default "false"; the same rules as `SENDS`).
